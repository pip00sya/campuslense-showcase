# Pilot Expenses

[Overview](README.md) · [Pilot criteria](METRICS.md#pilot-criteria)

The summary reports pilot expenses as a **$30–70 range**, against a target of **no more than $100**. The upper end of the reported range meets the budget criterion.

The source range is preserved. A single exact total cannot replace it without a breakdown of charges. Provider invoices and payment records are outside this package. Current prices and additional tariff calculations are not presented as pilot expenses.

Source: the summary's final criteria table, page 26. [Criteria CSV](data/success-criteria.csv), [source information](data/provenance.json).
